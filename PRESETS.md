# Factory presets

## Clean Start

Low wear starting point; no added noise.

## Soft Focus

Subtle motion and gentle saturation for a bus or keys.

## Warm VHS

Rounded video-tape colour with modest movement.

## Worn Cassette

More obvious movement, light noise and occasional wear.

## Rhodes Drive

Driven electric piano: strong saturation, slight motion, reduced output and no added noise.

## Noisy Rhodes

Electric piano drive with audible hiss, light crackle and uneven tape movement.

## Wide Memories

Stereo motion for sustained material; use on a stereo insert.

## Cliff Edge

Unstable playback and pronounced failures for transitions.

## Dusty Loop

Dusty colour for drum loops, with light hiss and modest instability.

## Classic Haze

Classic engine with its LP/HP and generation controls.

## Velvet Keys

Soft electric piano or pads with gentle warmth and slow pitch movement.

## Chorus Drift

Wide slow tape movement for sustained chords and synth pads.

## Crunchy Drums

Strong saturation with restrained pitch movement for drum loops.

## Old Radio

Classic-mode band-limited texture for voices, keys and sampled phrases.

## Broken Reel

Heavy irregular wear and drifting pitch for short transition effects.

These are starting points designed from the controls, not matched recordings of a physical pedal. Adjust Volume for your source. Host insert bypass remains separate. Tape Stop and pedal Aux controls are intentionally omitted.
